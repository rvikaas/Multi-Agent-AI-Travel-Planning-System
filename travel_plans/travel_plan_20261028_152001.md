## PRACTICAL TIPS TO STAY ON BUDGET

1. **Book transport early** – low‑cost airlines and night‑buses release their cheapest seats 60‑90 days ahead.
2. **Use a prepaid IC card** (Suica, Pasmo, ICOCA). Load ¥2,000 and you’ll never need to buy single tickets; you can also use the same card on buses and some convenience‑store purchases.
3. **Free Wi‑Fi** – most stations, cafes, and public parks have free Wi‑Fi, so you won’t need a data‑roaming plan.
4. **Cash is king** – many cheap eateries and street stalls only accept cash. Withdraw ¥10,000 (≈ $70) at the airport; you’ll get a good rate.
5. **Convenience‑store hacks** – 7‑Eleven, FamilyMart, and Lawson sell **ready‑to‑eat meals** for ¥100‑¥400. Look for “**おにぎり**” (rice balls), “**カップ麺**” (cup noodles), and “**サンドイッチ**”.
6. **Travel light** – most low‑cost airlines charge for checked bags. Pack a **carry‑on only** (max 7 kg) to avoid fees.
7. **Night‑bus safety** – bring a small travel pillow, earplugs, and a light blanket. Most buses have reclining seats and a small restroom on board.
8. **Cultural etiquette** – remove shoes when entering temples, keep noise low on public transport, and respect queue lines.
